Product

Growth debt, explained clearly

The goal of financing is growth. We expand beyond narrow venture debt so high-growth companies can raise flexible credit matched to their trajectory.

Is this for you?

We partner with innovative companies at an inflection point who want customised financing, not a template.

  • Revenue$5-150MM, or asset portfolios of $5MM+
  • Loans$1-50MM
  • StructuresTerm loans, warehouse facilities, lines of credit, royalty or revenue-based financing
  • SectorsSoftware, fintech, healthcare, infrastructure, hardware, consumer tech
  • SecurityTypically security in assets (senior or subordinate), equity participation, and financial covenants

What you can use it for

  • Scale product, marketing, and sales
  • Enter new geographies
  • Finance receivables or specific asset pools
  • Accelerate the path to profitability
  • Bridge strategic needs without heavy dilution

Growth debt vs venture debt

Similar benefits (lower cost than equity, less dilution). Different when, how, and in what form capital is offered.

Lens Typical venture debt Growth debt (PFG)
Stage Pre-revenue to Series B Post-revenue growth stages
Underwriting Often ownership / syndicate led Company credit, assets, profitability path
Backers Familiar VC validation VC, PE, family office, or bootstrapped
Timing Often with an equity round Tied to strategic priorities
Structures Term and royalty loans Term, royalty, revolvers, asset-backed / warehouse

Asset-backed expertise

Fintech and specialty finance often need facilities against receivables. We have deployed warehouse funding across currencies, asset classes, and jurisdictions.

Consumer

Corporate

Proptech
