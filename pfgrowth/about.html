Who we are

Partners to builders

For 35 years, the team behind PFG has worked at the heart of global tech. We unlock capital that fuels growth.

Our belief

Innovation arises where diverse perspectives meet hard problems. That shapes our team and how we work with founders and institutions.

Our story

Founded in 2004 by Andrew Kahn and Don Campbell, drawing on Hambrecht & Quist Guaranty Finance. As technology evolved, so did our structures: growth debt and asset-backed solutions for companies outside a narrow bank or venture-debt box.

At a glance

  • Offices across Americas, ANZ, MENA, and more
  • Strategic ties with SVB, IFC, and IDB Invest
