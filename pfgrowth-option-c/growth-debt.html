Growth debt, without noise

Flexible credit for high-growth companies. Matched to trajectory, not a template.

Is this for you?

Innovative companies at an inflection. Custom financing, not a one-size loan.

  • Revenue$5-150MM, or asset portfolios of $5MM+
  • Loans$1-50MM
  • StructuresTerm loans, warehouse facilities, lines of credit, royalty or revenue-based financing
  • SectorsSoftware, fintech, healthcare, infrastructure, hardware, consumer tech
  • SecurityTypically assets (senior or subordinate), equity participation, and financial covenants

Use cases

  • Scale product, marketing, and sales
  • Enter new geographies
  • Finance receivables or asset pools
  • Accelerate the path to profitability
  • Bridge needs without heavy dilution
