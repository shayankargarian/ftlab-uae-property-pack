1 · Plain English

What T02 actually is

Software for Abu Dhabi building managers and owners’ associations to run fees, ledgers, e-voting, and ADREC-facing reporting. Driven by 2025-26 strata governance rules, not another PropTech portal.

Category: RegTech SaaS for jointly owned property. Beachhead customer: ADREC-accredited management companies and OC boards in Abu Dhabi. Trigger channel: developers at first-unit handover (30-day appointment clock).
  • Why it showed up: Decision 25/26 package rewrote management and OC rules. Electronic management/accounting and six-month Centre reports are now Proven obligations.
  • What it is not: A Dubai escrow clone, a listings app, a full IFM suite, or a payments company in v1.
  • Market colour (not software TAM): ADREC YE2025 AED 142bn and H1 2026 AED 117bn transaction scale are Proven backdrop only.
