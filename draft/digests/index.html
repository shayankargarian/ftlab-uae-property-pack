Read this as a desk brief, not a brochure. Dubai has the deepest escrow / RERA process layer. Abu Dhabi now has operative jointly-owned-property and owners-committee text on the shelf (Decisions 25 and 26). Software substitutes already exist for generic OA and developer ERP. White space, if any, is Abu Dhabi-specific Centre workflow, still unproven by third-party evidence. Management-company headcount is still Unknown (E051).

The industry in one pass

Land and master planning feed construction. Off-plan sales sit on project escrow. Title, brokerage, and mortgages follow. Occupancy and facilities management then carry service charges, owners’ associations, and bilingual ops. Software money is smaller than land uplift and portals, but it grows where compliance is mandatory: escrow ledgers, statutory OA reporting, electronic accounting.

Three emirates, three rulebooks. Dubai DLD / RERA / Oqood / Ejari / Mollak. Abu Dhabi ADREC / DMT / DARI. Sharjah SRERD plus a still-Soft escrow pathway. Cross-emirate owners live in checklists and counsel. Industrial comps and escrow-progress APIs that contractors could call mostly do not exist in public.

Regulator pressure that is Proven on the shelf: Dubai Laws 8/2007 and 16/2007; Al Sa’fat 154/2020; DMT package announcement; Decision 25 Arts 20(6), 23(4), 24; Decision 26 Arts 2-5; ADREC 2025 and H1 2026 aggregates; DLD Project 360 launch 3 Sep 2026. The 28 Feb 2026 “effective date” is Soft counsel (E084). Primary Decisions say effective from Official Gazette publication. Gazette issue still not found.

Where the chain still improvises

Value chain. Land / master planning → development / construction → off-plan sales and escrow → title → brokerage and leasing → mortgage → occupancy and FM → asset management → capital markets → redevelopment.

Persistent gaps (who feels them): escrow-progress opacity (buyers, banks); service-charge / strata governance (owners, OCs); mid-market leasing on WhatsApp and Excel; no CoStar-class industrial comps; construction claims on legacy PM; cross-emirate compliance; thin valuation outside prime residential; green-ops vs design stamps; snagging at handover scale; Arabic document automation.

Negative space named in the gap digest: investor cockpit across DLD + ADREC; queryable industrial lease comps; escrow milestone API; OC treasury matching Abu Dhabi unified bylaws; photo progress ledger banks would accept; verified broker-performance market.
