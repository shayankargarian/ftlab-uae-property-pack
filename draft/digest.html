1 · Value chain

System map

Compressed industry map from digest-system-map.md (source 02-industry-system-map.md). Mobile flowchart TB.

flowchart TB
  L[Land / master planning] --> D[Development / construction]
  D --> O[Off-plan sales and escrow]
  O --> T[Title registration]
  T --> B[Brokerage and leasing]
  B --> M[Mortgage / finance]
  M --> F[Occupancy and FM]
  F --> A[Asset management / valuation]
  A --> C[Capital markets]
  C --> R[Redevelopment]

Actor layers

Land and planning Sovereign landowners; master developers; free-zone authorities; municipal planning (Dubai Municipality, DMT, Sharjah).
Construction Main contractors, MEP, progress certifiers who unlock escrow.
Regulation and title Dubai DLD / RERA / Oqood / Ejari; Abu Dhabi ADREC / DMT / DARI; Sharjah SRERD plus escrow reforms.
Demand generation Listing portals; brokerages; conveyancers; bank valuation panels.
Capital Retail / HNW buyers; mortgage banks; family offices; DIFC / ADGM managers.
Operations FM firms; owners’ associations / committees; property managers; BMS / IoT vendors.
Data and software DLD open data (strongest); portal data; escrow ledgers; local PropTech; global PM / construction suites with localisation gaps.
Bottlenecks that matter for software. Escrow-construction progress coupling; handover / snagging when off-plan share is high; fragmented mid-market leasing; cross-emirate rule fragmentation; thin institutional data outside Dubai residential; service-charge / OC governance (explicitly targeted by Abu Dhabi Decisions 25/26).

Money concentrates in land / development uplift and portals / brokerage. Software pools are smaller but grow with handover volume and compliance complexity.

Regulatory pressure (descriptive). Abu Dhabi Decisions 24/25/26/165 (2025) raise the bar on escrow early release, jointly owned property management systems, owners’ committees, and cancel / resale refunds. Dubai escrow / RERA remains the deepest institutional process layer. Sharjah escrow is maturing after Res. 37/2024; enforcement KPIs remain thin in open sources.

Decision callouts 24 / 25 / 26 / 165

Decision 24/2025
Early escrow disbursement controls before 20% completion. Primary PDF on shelf (SRC-P04). Ledger E013 still Soft until operative bank-guarantee clauses are quoted.
Decision 25/2025
Jointly owned property. Art.20(6) 30-day Centre-accredited management-company appointment; Art.23(4) six-month ADREC reports; Art.24 electronic management / accounting. Proven E067. Effective date from Gazette (Art.45), not 28 Feb.
Decision 26/2025
Unified Owners’ Committee bylaws. Formation at ≥30% multi-owned units; 5-9 resident members; developer excluded; electronic voting, one vote per participating owner. Proven E068.
Decision 165/2025
Compensation / refund timelines on cancelled and resold off-plan units. Package announcement Proven via E012; primary PDF on shelf (SRC-P05).

Cross-cutting: PDPL Decree-Law 45/2021 (owner-portal design constraint); Emiratisation labour rules (indirect FM productivity pressure).

6 · Negative space

What should exist but mostly does not

From the structural gap digest. Observational list for the industry desk.

  • Investor cockpit spanning DLD + ADREC portfolios (service charges, tenancy, insurance).
  • Public, queryable industrial lease comps.
  • Escrow milestone API usable by contractors, insurers, and software vendors.
  • OC / OA treasury with statutory reporting matching Abu Dhabi’s unified bylaws.
  • Progress-certified photo ledger acceptable to banks / regulators.
  • Secondary market for verified broker performance (still informal reputation).
Localisation note. Heavy imports: global PM suites, BMS hardware, construction software cores. Localisable surfaces: escrow logic, service-charge statutory forms, Arabic UX, industrial data aggregation, bilingual document AI.
